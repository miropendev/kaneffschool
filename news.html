25 October 2026

An invitation to the 20th anniversary of St. Dimitar

The school shared an invitation to the 20th anniversary of the consecration of St. Dimitar Bulgarian Eastern Orthodox Church, held on Sunday, 25 October 2026, at 10:00 a.m. at the church, 1555 Steeles Ave W, Brampton. Lunch and a concert followed at 12:30 p.m. at Lionhead Golf Club and Conference Centre, 8525 Mississauga Road, Brampton. Lunch was $30 a person. The day also marked the 100th birthday of Ignat Kaneff, named on the invitation as founder and benefactor. Guests were asked to reply by 11 October 2026. The poster includes a QR code for that reply.

30 June 2026

The summer celebration brought students, parents, and friends together

With many smiles and a great deal of cheer, Bulgarian Sunday School Kaneff marked the end of the school year with its traditional summer celebration. Students presented songs, poems, dances, and short plays drawn from Bulgarian history, culture, and tradition. Each class showed what it had learned, and parents and guests applauded. The day was also a chance to gather as a community, to share the joy of the year, and to thank the teachers, parents, and volunteers. After the programme, guests talked, tasted Bulgarian food, and the children kept playing. Thank you to everyone who was part of this day. We look forward to the new school year, and to many more moments together.
