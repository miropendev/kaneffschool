Bulgarian Sunday School Kaneff Est. 2004

A home away from home

Bulgarian Sunday School Kaneff is where Bulgarian families find community, support, and the feeling of home, however far they are from Bulgaria. What matters most to us is that every child arrives wanting to be here, learns with a smile, and is proud to be Bulgarian.

We teach children of different ages on programmes approved by the Ministry of Education and Science of the Republic of Bulgaria, joining sound schooling with creativity, games, and feasts.

Our mission

To keep and pass on the Bulgarian language, culture, history, and traditions to the next generations of Bulgarians living in Canada.

We are building a community where children feel tied to their homeland, and parents find support and a place to share Bulgarian values with their families.

Enrolment

Come into the school family.

Families can join during the year. Tell us about your child and we will help you find the right group. The parents’ council works with the teachers, so the school stays a partnership with home.
