Learning

Programmes shaped to each child.

Lessons follow curricula affirmed by the Ministry of Education and Science. Students use textbooks written for Bulgarian schools abroad. The day mixes clear teaching with projects, play, and practice, matched to each child’s age and knowledge.

Groups

  1. Preparatory group
  2. Primary · Grades 1–4
  3. Lower secondary · Grades 5–7
  4. Upper secondary · Grades 8–12

Subjects

  • Bulgarian language and literature
  • History and civilisations
  • Geography of Bulgaria
  • Workshops, feasts, customs, and folk dance

How children attend

In person

At our Mississauga, Brampton, and Niagara Falls campuses.

Online

Live distance lessons for children elsewhere in Canada, and anywhere in the world.

Individual support

Extra help matched to what a child already knows.

Kaneff is on the Ministry of Education and Science list of Bulgarian Sunday schools abroad. After a successful year, students receive official documents under the Ministry’s rules. Students may also sit an online Bulgarian exam aligned with the Common European Framework of Reference.
