About

More than a school. A community.

Kaneff keeps the Bulgarian language, culture, history, and traditions alive for children growing up in Canada, and gives their parents a place to stand with them.

Our mission

To keep and pass on the Bulgarian language, culture, history, and traditions to the next generations of Bulgarians living in Canada.

We are building a community where children feel tied to their homeland, and parents find support and a place to share Bulgarian values with their families.

Every child deserves to know their roots. With love, patience, and inspiration we help Bulgarian children in Canada discover the language, the history, and the traditions, and to make friends and memories along the way.

Our history

A bridge since 2004.

Kaneff began in 2004 at the Bulgarian Orthodox Church of St. Dimitar in Brampton, as a bridge between Bulgaria and Bulgarian families in Canada. Children learn the language, keep the feasts, and build a lasting tie to who they are.

Why us

We teach children to love Bulgaria.

Grammar and spelling are only the start. We teach children to respect their traditions and to be confident in who they are.

Qualified, devoted teachers

Professional experience, and a real love of working with children growing up outside Bulgaria.

A friendly, supportive circle

Children make friends. Parents find other Bulgarian families, and a place that feels like theirs.

Language, literature, history, geography

Bulgarian language and literature, history and civilisations, and the geography of Bulgaria.

Workshops and folk dance

Making, customs, and dance, so the language is something children use, not only a subject.

Feasts for the whole community

Concerts, celebrations, and gatherings that bring students, parents, and teachers together.

In the room, and online

In-person classes across Greater Toronto and Niagara, and distance learning for children anywhere.

Our teachers

Our teachers are the heart of the school. They bring professional experience and a love of working with children, and they keep the classroom calm, friendly, and full of reason to try.

Parents’ council

A good education is a partnership between the school and the family. The parents’ council helps with feasts, cultural events, charity efforts, and school projects. Because parents take part, Kaneff is a community, not only a classroom.

Rooms for real lessons

The rooms are bright and comfortable, with what a modern lesson needs. To stay near Bulgarian families, the school keeps three campuses: Mississauga, Brampton, and Niagara Falls.
