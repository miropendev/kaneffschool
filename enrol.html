Enrol

Enrolment

Come into the school family.

Families can join during the year. Tell us about your child and we will help you find the right group. The parents’ council works with the teachers, so the school stays a partnership with home.

Annual fee

  • First child

    550 CAD

  • Second child in the family

    450 CAD

  • Third child and each one after

    Free

Ask us to confirm this year’s fee before you pay. It can be paid in full, or in two equal parts at the start of each term.

How to pay

  • e-Transfer to bulgariansundayschoolkanev@gmail.com
  • Or in person at the school.
  • Two equal instalments are welcome: one at the start of the first term, one at the start of the second.

Kaneff is on the Ministry of Education and Science list of Bulgarian Sunday schools abroad. After a successful year, students receive official documents under the Ministry’s rules. Students may also sit an online Bulgarian exam aligned with the Common European Framework of Reference.

Questions about enrolment, the programme, the campuses, or how a child attends are welcome. The school team will be glad to help.

How to begin

  1. Write to us with your child’s three names in Cyrillic, date of birth, and preferred campus.
  2. We suggest a group, from the preparatory class through grade 12, in person or online.
  3. Pay the annual fee by e-Transfer or at the school.

Photographs and video

From time to time the school photographs and films students during lessons, feasts, workshops, and other school activities. With your consent, those pictures may be used for school information, including this website, the school’s social pages, newsletters, printed materials, and reports. The student’s full name, address, phone number, and other personal details are not published without a further explicit consent. Consent is voluntary and can be withdrawn in writing. Withdrawal does not affect material already published.
